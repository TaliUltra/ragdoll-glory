How to Take Care of Your Ragdoll Kitten

Feeding

Kittens up to 3 months old should be fed 4–5 times a day. From 3–6 months, feed 3 times a day, and after 6 months, 2 meals a day. Always provide fresh water and high-quality kitten food rich in proteins.

Grooming

Brush your kitten 2–3 times a week to prevent tangles and hairballs. Bath only when necessary with kitten-safe shampoo. Grooming strengthens your bond.

Litter Box

Clean the litter box daily and change litter weekly. Place it in a quiet, accessible area and use unscented clumping litter.

Handling & Petting

Pet your kitten gently along the back, cheeks, and under the chin. Avoid grabbing the tail or rough play. Regular handling builds trust.

Playtime

Use interactive toys like feather wands or balls. Play 2–3 times a day for 10–15 minutes to keep your kitten active and happy.

Sleeping

Provide a warm, quiet, and comfortable bed. Kittens sleep up to 18 hours a day.

Claw Care

Provide scratching posts and trim claws every 2–3 weeks. Never declaw surgically.

Health & Socialization

Regular vet check-ups, vaccinations, and gentle introduction to people and pets make your kitten confident and well-adjusted.

Adaptation in a New Home

Give your kitten a quiet, safe space to explore. Introduce them gradually to people, pets, and new surroundings. Provide familiar items like a bed, toys, and a scratching post. Be patient, gentle, and consistent—most kittens settle in within a few days.
